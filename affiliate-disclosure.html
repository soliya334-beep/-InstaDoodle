Our Affiliate Disclosure

This website is an independent review site about InstaDoodle. We are not the creator or the seller of the software. Some of the links on our pages are affiliate links, which means that if you click one and then make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the seller or the payment marketplace.

Why We Tell You This

Consumer protection authorities in many countries, including the Federal Trade Commission in the United States, expect websites that earn commissions to disclose the relationship clearly. We would do it anyway. You deserve to know how a recommendation is funded, so that you can weigh it properly.

You will find a short disclosure line near the top of our main page and in the footer of every page, and the buttons that lead to the order page are marked as sponsored links.

How It Affects Our Content

A commission creates an obvious incentive to be positive, and we want to be upfront about the ways we try to manage that.

  • We describe InstaDoodle as it is, including the complaints in public reviews and the gaps in its own sales pages.
  • We point out where the vendor’s numbers and wording disagree.
  • We publish no invented reviews, ratings or results.
  • We explain the refund process in detail, even though a refund means we earn nothing.
  • We include alternatives and free options so that you can decide not to buy at all.
