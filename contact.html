Quick answer

Email support@instadoodleai.com for questions about this website. For refunds, orders and accounts, contact InstaDoodle support at help.instadoodle.com, because this site cannot see or change orders.

Contact This Website

You can reach the team behind this independent review site by email at support@instadoodleai.com. We read every message about the website, including corrections, suggestions and questions about our guides. Please allow a few days for a reply.

We are not the seller of InstaDoodle. We cannot process refunds, change plans or see your order or account. Please read the next section for the right place to go.

Refunds, Orders and Account Problems

InstaDoodle is sold and supported by its vendor. If you bought it and need a refund, cannot log in, see a charge you do not recognise or have a billing question, the right place is the vendor’s help centre at help.instadoodle.com. Open a ticket with your order number and the email address you used at checkout.

If your receipt shows that a marketplace such as ClickBank processed your payment, that marketplace may also offer an order-support site where you can look up your order and request a refund during the refund period. Our refund and free trial guide explains each step.

What We Can Help With

  • Questions about anything written on this website.
  • Corrections to facts, dates or links.
  • Suggestions for topics you would like us to cover.
  • Sharing your own experience of InstaDoodle so we can improve our guides. Please say how long you used it and what you made. We will not publish your name without your permission.

What We Cannot Help With

  • Questions about your personal order, subscription or account.
  • Legal, financial or professional advice.
  • Anything involving your card or bank account.

Response Times and Privacy

We aim to reply within a few working days, although busy periods can take longer. Please do not send passwords, full card numbers or sensitive personal details by email. If you write to us, we use your message only to reply and to improve our guides. We do not sell email addresses, and we do not add anyone to a mailing list because they contacted us.

Helpful Details to Include

A short message with the right details gets a faster answer. If you are reporting a mistake, tell us the page, the sentence and what you believe is correct, ideally with a link to a public source. If you are suggesting a topic, tell us what you were trying to find out and what you could not find. If you are sharing a question about a purchase, remember that we cannot see orders, so please send it to the seller instead. Messages written in plain language, with one clear question each, are easiest for us to handle and to answer well.

Before You Write to Us

It may save time to check our guides first. Many questions are answered in the reviews guide, the legitimacy check and the main InstaDoodle review.
